About the EFCC

The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) is the federal law enforcement agency responsible for investigating, prosecuting, and preventing economic and financial crimes in Nigeria. It was established by the EFCC (Establishment) Act of 2004.

The EFCC is at the forefront of Nigeria's fight against corruption, money laundering, fraud, and other financial crimes. It works to sanitize the financial system, recover stolen assets, and promote transparency and accountability in public and private sectors.

Core Functions

  • Investigate economic and financial crimes.
  • Prosecute offenders involved in financial crimes.
  • Prevent money laundering and terrorist financing.
  • Recover proceeds of crime and stolen assets.
  • Monitor financial transactions for suspicious activities.
  • Collaborate with international law enforcement agencies.
  • Promote public awareness of economic crimes.
  • Enforce financial regulations and anti-corruption laws.
  • Provide intelligence on financial crime trends.

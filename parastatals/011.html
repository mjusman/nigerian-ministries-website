About the NCC

The Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) is the independent regulatory authority for the telecommunications industry in Nigeria. Established by the Nigerian Communications Act of 2003, the NCC is responsible for creating an enabling environment for competition, protecting consumers, and promoting the growth of the telecoms sector.

The NCC licenses telecom operators, manages the spectrum, sets quality standards, and ensures that telecommunications services are accessible, affordable, and reliable for all Nigerians.

Core Functions

  • Regulate the telecommunications industry.
  • License telecommunications operators and service providers.
  • Manage the radio frequency spectrum.
  • Protect consumers from unfair practices.
  • Promote competition and market efficiency.
  • Set technical standards and quality of service requirements.
  • Facilitate broadband penetration and digital inclusion.
  • Resolve disputes between operators and consumers.
  • Advise the government on communications policy.

About NITDA

The National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) is the federal agency responsible for developing, regulating, and promoting information technology in Nigeria. Established by the NITDA Act of 2007, the Agency works to create an enabling environment for IT growth and digital innovation.

NITDA is at the forefront of Nigeria's digital transformation agenda, promoting digital literacy, supporting IT startups, and ensuring that government services are delivered efficiently through technology.

Core Functions

  • Develop and regulate information technology in Nigeria.
  • Promote digital literacy and IT skills development.
  • Support IT innovation and entrepreneurship.
  • Set IT standards and guidelines for the public sector.
  • Coordinate e-government initiatives.
  • Promote local IT content and software development.
  • Enforce IT project clearance for government agencies.
  • Conduct IT research and development.
  • Protect critical national information infrastructure.

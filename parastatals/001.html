Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)

Nigeria's Federal Tax Administration Authority

Overview

The Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) is the principal agency of the Federal Government of Nigeria responsible for assessing, collecting, and accounting for taxes and other revenues due to the Federal Government. The Service plays a vital role in financing national development by ensuring efficient tax administration, improving tax compliance, and expanding the country's non-oil revenue base.

Vision

To be a globally recognized tax authority that delivers excellent tax administration through innovation, transparency, and voluntary compliance.

Mission

To provide an efficient and effective tax system that promotes voluntary compliance, supports economic growth, and contributes significantly to Nigeria's sustainable development.

Core Responsibilities

  • Assessing and collecting federal taxes.
  • Administering tax laws enacted by the National Assembly.
  • Promoting voluntary tax compliance among taxpayers.
  • Registering taxpayers and maintaining tax records.
  • Conducting tax audits and investigations.
  • Providing taxpayer education and support services.
  • Collaborating with other government agencies to improve revenue generation.
  • Leveraging technology to simplify tax administration.

Major Taxes Administered

Tax Description
Companies Income Tax (CIT) Tax on the profits of companies operating in Nigeria.
Value Added Tax (VAT) Consumption tax on goods and services.
Petroleum-related Taxes Taxes applicable to petroleum and upstream operations.
Capital Gains Tax Tax on gains arising from the disposal of chargeable assets.
Stamp Duties Tax charged on specified legal instruments and transactions.
Withholding Tax An advance payment of income tax deducted at source.

Strategic Objectives

  • Increase tax compliance nationwide.
  • Enhance taxpayer experience through digital services.
  • Expand Nigeria's tax base.
  • Improve transparency and accountability.
  • Strengthen revenue collection efficiency.
  • Support economic growth through effective tax policies.

Digital Services

FIRS provides several online services that enable taxpayers to:

  • Register for Tax Identification Numbers (TIN).
  • File tax returns electronically.
  • Pay taxes online.
  • Verify tax records.
  • Access taxpayer support and guidance.

Importance to Nigeria

The Federal Inland Revenue Service is a key institution in Nigeria's public finance system. Revenue collected by the Service helps fund national infrastructure, education, healthcare, security, transportation, agriculture, and other public services. By encouraging tax compliance and improving revenue collection, FIRS contributes to the country's economic stability and sustainable development.

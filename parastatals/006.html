Nigerian Identity Management Commission (NIMC)

The Nigerian Identity Management Commission (NIMC) is the federal agency responsible for managing Nigeria's national identity system and issuing the National Identification Number (NIN) to citizens and legal residents.

About NIMC

The Nigerian Identity Management Commission (NIMC) was established by the NIMC Act No. 23 of 2007 to create, own, operate, maintain, and manage Nigeria's National Identity Database. The Commission is responsible for registering citizens and legal residents and assigning each person a unique National Identification Number (NIN).

Mission: To establish and operate a reliable, secure, and inclusive national identity management system that supports governance, security, service delivery, and economic development.

Core Functions

  • Register Nigerian citizens and legal residents.
  • Issue the National Identification Number (NIN).
  • Maintain the National Identity Database.
  • Issue the National e-ID Card and other identity credentials where applicable.
  • Verify and authenticate identities for government agencies and authorized organizations.
  • Coordinate identity management across government institutions.
  • Support digital identity initiatives and secure identity verification.
  • Promote an integrated national identity management system.

Services Offered

National Identification Number (NIN)

The National Identification Number (NIN) is a unique 11-digit number assigned to every enrolled individual. It serves as a permanent identity number and is used across various government and private sector services.

Importance of the NIN

  • Identity verification.
  • Passport applications.
  • Driver's licence processing.
  • SIM registration and linkage.
  • Bank account verification.
  • Government social intervention programmes.
  • Tax and public service administration.
  • Access to various public and private sector services.

Objectives

  • Create a unified national identity system.
  • Improve national security through reliable identity verification.
  • Reduce identity fraud and duplication.
  • Support efficient government service delivery.
  • Enable digital transformation and e-government initiatives.
  • Promote financial inclusion and economic development.

Benefits to Nigerians

  • Secure and verifiable identity.
  • Easier access to government services.
  • Simplified banking and financial services.
  • Support for digital transactions.
  • Improved access to healthcare and education programmes.
  • Reduced identity theft and fraud.

Official Contact Information

For enrollment, identity verification, NIN modifications, and other services, visit the official Nigerian Identity Management Commission website or any accredited enrollment centre nationwide.

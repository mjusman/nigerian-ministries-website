About NIMASA

The Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency (NIMASA) is the federal agency responsible for regulating and promoting maritime safety, security, and shipping development in Nigeria. It was established by the NIMASA Act, No. 25 of 2007.

NIMASA works to ensure that Nigeria's maritime domain is safe for navigation, secure from threats, and contributes significantly to the national economy through shipping, port operations, and related maritime activities.

Core Functions

  • Regulate maritime safety and security.
  • Promote Nigerian shipping and maritime commerce.
  • Implement international maritime conventions.
  • Register and license Nigerian ships and vessels.
  • Conduct maritime search and rescue operations.
  • Protect the marine environment from pollution.
  • Develop indigenous shipping capacity.
  • Train and certify maritime personnel.
  • Coordinate maritime domain awareness.

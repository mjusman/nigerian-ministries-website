About the NPC

The National Population Commission (NPC) is the federal agency responsible for conducting censuses, collecting demographic data, and managing Nigeria's population register. Established by Decree No. 23 of 1989 (now an Act of the National Assembly).

The NPC provides accurate and reliable population data that guides government planning, resource allocation, policy formulation, and research across all sectors of the Nigerian economy.

Core Functions

  • Conduct national population and housing censuses.
  • Collect, analyse, and publish demographic data.
  • Maintain the National Population Register.
  • Provide demographic data for planning and development.
  • Conduct surveys on population and health indicators.
  • Monitor population trends and projections.
  • Collaborate with international statistical organizations.
  • Promote awareness of population issues.
  • Advise the government on population policies.

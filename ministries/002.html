About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Education is responsible for formulating and implementing national policies that promote quality education throughout Nigeria. The Ministry oversees educational development, coordinates federal education institutions, and works with state governments and other stakeholders to improve learning outcomes.

Mission: To provide accessible, inclusive, and high-quality education that supports national development and prepares citizens with the knowledge and skills needed for the future.

Vision

To build a world-class education system that empowers every Nigerian learner through quality teaching, innovation, research, and lifelong learning.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop national education policies.
  • Coordinate federal educational institutions.
  • Improve access to quality education.
  • Promote science, technology, and innovation in education.
  • Support teacher education and professional development.
  • Monitor educational standards nationwide.
  • Collaborate with state governments and development partners.

Major Departments

  • Basic and Secondary Education
  • Higher Education
  • Technical and Vocational Education
  • Educational Planning, Research and Development
  • Quality Assurance Services
  • Human Resources Management
  • Finance and Accounts

Key Initiatives

The Ministry supports various educational initiatives aimed at improving literacy, expanding digital learning, strengthening technical and vocational education, improving teacher capacity, and increasing access to education for all Nigerians.

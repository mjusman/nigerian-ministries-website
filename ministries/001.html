Federal Ministry of Finance, Nigeria

Promoting Economic Growth and Financial Stability

About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Finance is one of the key ministries of the Federal Government of Nigeria. It is responsible for managing the nation's public finances, formulating fiscal policies, and ensuring sustainable economic development.

Core Responsibilities

  • Developing and implementing fiscal policies.
  • Preparing and managing the national budget.
  • Managing government revenue and expenditure.
  • Coordinating public debt management.
  • Promoting transparency and accountability in public finance.
  • Supporting economic growth through sound financial management.

Mission

To ensure effective management of Nigeria's financial resources, promote fiscal discipline, and create an enabling environment for sustainable economic growth and national development.

Vision

To build a strong, stable, and inclusive economy through prudent financial management and responsible fiscal governance.

Key Areas of Focus

  • Economic policy formulation
  • Budget implementation
  • Revenue generation
  • Public financial management reforms
  • International financial cooperation
  • Investment promotion

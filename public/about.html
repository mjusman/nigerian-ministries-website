Who We Are

Nigerian Info is an independent educational website dedicated to providing accurate, well-organized, and easy-to-understand information about the Federal Government institutions of Nigeria. We cover Federal Ministries, Government Agencies, Parastatals, Commissions, and other public institutions that serve the Nigerian people.

Our mission is to promote civic education by helping Nigerians and the global community better understand how Nigeria's government is structured and how its various institutions function. We believe that an informed citizenry is essential for good governance and national development.

Our Mission

To educate and inform by publishing factual, concise, and accessible content about Nigeria's federal government institutions, their mandates, functions, and contributions to national development.

Our Vision

To become Nigeria's most trusted online resource for government institutional information, helping bridge the knowledge gap between citizens and their government.

What We Cover

  • Federal Ministries — Policy-making bodies of the executive arm of government
  • Parastatals & Agencies — Specialized government organizations delivering public services
  • Commissions — Regulatory and oversight bodies
  • Government-Owned Companies — Commercial entities owned by the federal government
  • Public Services — Information about government programs and citizen services

Our Values

  • Accuracy — We verify information using official government sources
  • Independence — We are not affiliated with any government or political entity
  • Transparency — We clearly state our sources and limitations
  • Accessibility — We present information in clear, simple language
  • Objectivity — We present facts without political or regional bias

Our Editorial Standards

All content published on Nigerian Info undergoes editorial review for accuracy, clarity, and relevance. We consult official government publications, acts of the National Assembly, and publicly available records. When errors are identified, we correct them promptly and transparently.

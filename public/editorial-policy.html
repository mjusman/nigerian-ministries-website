Editorial Policy

Effective Date: July 19, 2026

Welcome to Nigerian Info (https://nigerian-info.web.app/). Our mission is to provide reliable, educational, and easy-to-understand information about Nigeria's ministries, government agencies, parastatals, laws, public institutions, and related public services.

This Editorial Policy explains how we create, review, and maintain the content published on our website. It reflects our commitment to accuracy, transparency, fairness, and editorial independence, following widely accepted digital publishing standards. We use these principles to keep our content accurate, transparent, useful, and clearly separated from official government sources.

1. Editorial Mission

Our goal is to educate Nigerians and the global community by publishing factual, well-organized, and easy-to-read information about:

  • Nigerian Federal Ministries
  • Government Agencies and Parastatals
  • Public Services
  • Nigerian Laws and Regulations
  • Government Programs and Policies
  • Civic Education
  • Official Procedures and Public Information

Our content is intended for informational and educational purposes only.

2. Accuracy

We strive to ensure that every article is accurate at the time of publication. Before publishing, our editorial team:

  • Consults official government publications whenever available.
  • Verifies facts using credible and publicly available sources.
  • Reviews content for clarity, grammar, and factual consistency.
  • Updates outdated information whenever significant changes occur.

Despite our best efforts, errors may occasionally occur. We welcome corrections from readers.

3. Sources of Information

Our articles may be based on information obtained from:

  • Official Nigerian government websites
  • Government publications
  • Acts of the National Assembly
  • Public records
  • Official press releases
  • Publicly available educational resources

Where appropriate, we encourage readers to verify important information directly with the relevant government authority.

4. Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions are made independently. Content is not influenced by advertisers, political organizations, government officials, or commercial partners.

Sponsored or promotional content, if published in the future, will always be clearly identified.

5. Fairness and Neutrality

We aim to present factual information objectively and without political, ethnic, religious, or regional bias.

Opinion pieces, if published, will be clearly labelled as opinions and will not be presented as factual reporting.

6. Content Review

Before publication, content may undergo editorial review for:

  • Accuracy
  • Grammar
  • Readability
  • Consistency
  • Compliance with applicable laws

7. Corrections Policy

If a factual error is identified:

  • We investigate the reported issue promptly.
  • Verified inaccuracies are corrected as soon as reasonably possible.
  • Major corrections may include an update notice where appropriate.

8. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Some content may be drafted or assisted using artificial intelligence tools. Every article is reviewed and edited by a human before publication to improve accuracy, clarity, and usefulness.

AI is used only as an editorial assistance tool and never replaces human editorial judgment.

9. User Feedback

We value constructive feedback from our readers. If you notice inaccurate, outdated, or misleading information, please contact us so we can review it.

10. Advertising and Monetization

To support the operation of this website, we may display advertisements, including Google AdSense advertisements.

Advertising does not influence our editorial decisions, content selection, or recommendations.

11. Content Updates

Government information may change over time. We periodically review our articles to ensure they remain accurate and relevant.

12. Editorial Standards

We are committed to:

  • Accuracy
  • Transparency
  • Independence
  • Fairness
  • Educational value
  • Reader trust

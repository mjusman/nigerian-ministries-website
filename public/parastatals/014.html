What is the National Population Commission?

The National Population Commission (NPC) is a constitutional Federal Executive Body responsible for important population and civil-registration functions in Nigeria. Its mandate includes conducting population enumeration, maintaining machinery for continuous and universal registration of births and deaths, advising on population matters, and providing population information for economic and development planning.

The Commission also works on census preparation, demographic surveys, migration statistics, population information systems and public education about its functions. Its work therefore goes beyond a census exercise: population and vital-event information can support planning in areas such as health, education, infrastructure and social services.

Legal and Institutional Basis

NPC states that its constitutional mandate is grounded in section 153(j) of the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. The Commission also operates under legislation governing its functions, including the National Population Commission Act and the law governing compulsory registration of births and deaths.

NPC has publicly described ongoing discussions about consolidating and modernising the legal framework for civil registration. A proposed or discussed reform should not be treated as enacted law unless confirmed through an authoritative legal source.

Core Functions

  • Undertake population enumeration through censuses, sample surveys or other approved methods.
  • Establish and maintain machinery for continuous and universal registration of births and deaths.
  • Provide population information and data for economic and development planning.
  • Advise the President on population matters.
  • Prepare and maintain frameworks used for census and survey delineation, including locality information and house-numbering work.
  • Collect, collate and publish relevant demographic and migration statistics.
  • Research and monitor population policy and maintain population information resources.
  • Educate the public about the Commission's functions and population-related information.
  • Train or arrange for the training of enumerators and Commission personnel as required.

Services and Public Information

NPC's current public-facing services include population and census activities as well as civil registration and vital-statistics services. The exact availability, requirements and charges of an online service can change, so users should verify details on the linked NPC portal before starting an application.

Birth registration and birth attestation

NPC provides digital and registration-centre pathways for birth registration and birth attestation. Its current FAQ states that birth registration is for children aged 0–17, while birth attestation is for individuals aged 18 and above. Requirements can include NIN-related information and, for birth attestation, a court-sworn affidavit.

Certificate reissuance, reprint and verification

NPC provides online pathways for services such as certificate reissuance and certificate reprint, and it maintains information about certificate verification. Requirements differ by service. For example, the official reissuance service states that applicants may need NIN information and an existing non-digital certificate.

Census, surveys and population data

The Commission's work includes census preparation and enumeration, enumeration-area demarcation, surveys, population management and publication of demographic information. Researchers, planners, institutions and members of the public can use official NPC publications and data where available.

Who Uses NPC Services and Information?

  • Parents and guardians: for birth registration and related civil-registration processes.
  • Adults: for applicable birth-attestation and certificate services.
  • Researchers and students: for population, demographic and survey information.
  • Government institutions: for population evidence used in planning and policy work.
  • Organisations and development partners: for relevant population and civil-registration information, subject to official access rules.

Important Information Before Using an NPC Service

  • Start from the official NPC website rather than an unofficial social-media link or third-party payment page.
  • Check the current requirements for the exact service you need before paying or submitting information.
  • Do not share NIN, identity documents, payment references or other sensitive information with an unverified website.
  • Keep application or payment references where the official service instructs you to do so.
  • For fees, service availability and processing instructions, rely on the current NPC service page because these details may change.

Relationship to the Nigerian Government

NPC is a federal constitutional commission. Its population and civil-registration work intersects with other public institutions because demographic and identity-related information can be used across government functions. NPC itself identifies collaboration with institutions including the National Identity Management Commission (NIMC), health authorities, state governments and local structures in its civil-registration work.

NPC should not be confused with NIMC. NPC focuses on population enumeration and civil registration within its mandate, while NIMC is responsible for the national identity-management framework. Their systems and responsibilities can intersect, particularly around birth records and identity information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NPC the same as NIMC?

No. They are separate federal institutions with different mandates. NPC handles population and civil-registration functions within its mandate; NIMC is responsible for national identity management.

What does NPC do besides a census?

Its mandate also covers birth and death registration, demographic information, surveys, population policy advice, census and survey frameworks, migration statistics and public education.

Can I apply for a birth-related service online?

NPC currently provides online pathways for services including birth registration, birth attestation and certificate-related services. Use the current service links on the official NPC website and check the applicable requirements before proceeding.

Where can I find an NPC state office?

NPC publishes a state-office directory on its official website. The addresses and contact details should be checked there rather than copied from an unofficial directory.

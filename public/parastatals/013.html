About NERC

The Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) is the independent regulatory agency responsible for the regulation of the Nigerian electricity industry. Established by the Electric Power Sector Reform Act (EPSRA) of 2005, NERC oversees the generation, transmission, distribution, and sale of electricity in Nigeria.

NERC ensures that electricity tariffs are fair, service quality is maintained, and the power sector operates efficiently and transparently for the benefit of consumers and investors.

Core Functions

  • Regulate the electricity industry in Nigeria.
  • Issue licences for generation, transmission, and distribution.
  • Set and review electricity tariffs.
  • Monitor service quality and consumer protection.
  • Promote competition in the electricity market.
  • Enforce compliance with electricity regulations.
  • Facilitate private sector investment in power.
  • Develop and enforce electricity codes and standards.
  • Resolve disputes between operators and consumers.

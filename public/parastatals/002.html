About NNPCL

The Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPCL) is Nigeria's national oil company responsible for participating in the exploration, production, refining, transportation, marketing, and trading of petroleum products. It plays a vital role in ensuring energy security, supporting economic development, and maximizing value from the nation's hydrocarbon resources.

Established: 1977 (as Nigerian National Petroleum Corporation - NNPC)
Commercialized: 2022 as Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited under the Petroleum Industry Act (PIA) 2021.
Headquarters: Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria.

History

The Nigerian National Petroleum Corporation (NNPC) was established in 1977 following the merger of the Nigerian National Oil Corporation (NNOC) and the Federal Ministry of Petroleum Resources. For decades, it managed government participation in Nigeria's petroleum industry.

Following the implementation of the Petroleum Industry Act (PIA) 2021, NNPC was transformed into the Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPCL) in July 2022. The transition was intended to improve commercial efficiency, transparency, competitiveness, and long-term sustainability.

Core Responsibilities

  • Oil and natural gas exploration.
  • Crude oil production and joint venture management.
  • Refining crude oil into petroleum products.
  • Natural gas development and commercialization.
  • Pipeline and depot operations.
  • Marketing and distribution of petroleum products.
  • International crude oil trading.
  • Investment in renewable and cleaner energy initiatives.
  • Supporting Nigeria's energy security and economic development.

Major Business Areas

Business Area Description
Upstream Exploration and production of crude oil and natural gas.
Midstream Gas processing, pipelines, transportation, and storage infrastructure.
Downstream Refining, fuel marketing, distribution, and retail operations.
Gas & Power Development of Nigeria's natural gas resources and power-related investments.
Trading Domestic and international crude oil and petroleum products trading.

Strategic Objectives

  • Increase crude oil and natural gas production.
  • Improve refinery performance and local refining capacity.
  • Expand gas utilization across Nigeria.
  • Attract local and foreign investment.
  • Promote operational excellence and transparency.
  • Support sustainable energy development.
  • Create long-term value for shareholders and the Nigerian economy.

Governance

NNPCL operates as a limited liability company under the Companies and Allied Matters Act (CAMA) and the Petroleum Industry Act (PIA). It is governed by a Board of Directors and executive management responsible for corporate strategy, operational performance, and regulatory compliance.

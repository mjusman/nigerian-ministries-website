About the Nigeria Customs Service

The Nigeria Customs Service (NCS) is the federal agency responsible for the administration and enforcement of customs and excise laws in Nigeria. It operates under the Federal Ministry of Finance and plays a critical role in trade facilitation, revenue generation, and border security.

The NCS collects customs duties, prevents smuggling, ensures compliance with trade regulations, and facilitates legitimate international trade through efficient clearance processes.

Core Functions

  • Collect customs duties and excise taxes.
  • Facilitate legitimate international trade.
  • Prevent smuggling and illegal importation.
  • Enforce customs and excise laws.
  • Protect Nigeria's borders from illegal goods.
  • Implement trade agreements and preferences.
  • Inspect and clear imported and exported goods.
  • Collaborate with international customs organizations.
  • Promote trade facilitation and automation.

About NAFDAC

The National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) is a federal government agency under the Federal Ministry of Health and Social Welfare. It is responsible for regulating and controlling the manufacture, importation, exportation, distribution, advertisement, sale, and use of food, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices, chemicals, detergents, packaged water, and other regulated products throughout Nigeria.

NAFDAC was established by Decree No. 15 of 1993 (now the NAFDAC Act, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria) to safeguard public health by ensuring that only safe, effective, and high-quality regulated products are available to consumers.

Core Functions

  • Regulates and controls the manufacture, importation, exportation, distribution, sale, and use of regulated products.
  • Registers food, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices, packaged water, chemicals, and detergents.
  • Inspects manufacturing facilities to ensure compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP).
  • Conducts laboratory analysis and quality testing of products.
  • Monitors adverse drug reactions and product safety.
  • Combats counterfeit, adulterated, expired, and substandard products.
  • Carries out public enlightenment and consumer education campaigns.
  • Collaborates with national and international regulatory organizations.
  • Enforces regulations through inspections, surveillance, investigations, and sanctions.

Products Regulated by NAFDAC

Category Description
Food Products Processed foods, beverages, bottled water, packaged foods, food additives, and related products.
Medicines Prescription medicines, over-the-counter medicines, vaccines, herbal medicines, and biological products.
Cosmetics Beauty products, skincare products, perfumes, soaps, and personal care items.
Medical Devices Medical equipment, diagnostic kits, hospital consumables, and related healthcare products.
Chemicals Industrial chemicals, household chemicals, detergents, disinfectants, and similar products.
Packaged Water Bottled water, sachet water, and other packaged drinking water products.

Services Provided

  • Product registration and certification.
  • Import permit processing.
  • Laboratory testing and analysis.
  • Facility inspection and licensing.
  • Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) inspections.
  • Public health awareness and education.
  • Investigation of counterfeit and fake products.
  • Consumer complaint handling.
  • Product recalls and safety alerts.

Mission

To safeguard public health by ensuring that only safe, wholesome, effective, and quality food, medicines, cosmetics, medical devices, chemicals, packaged water, and related products are manufactured, imported, exported, distributed, sold, and used in Nigeria.

Vision

To be a world-class regulatory agency that protects and promotes public health through effective regulation and control of regulated products in accordance with international best practices.

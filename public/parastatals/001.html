What is the Nigeria Revenue Service?

The Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) is Nigeria's federal revenue administration body. It succeeded the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) under the Nigeria Revenue Service (Establishment) Act, 2025, which commenced on 26 June 2025. The new framework took effect as part of the country's 2026 tax-administration reforms.

This page is an independent educational guide. It is not the official NRS website and should not be used as a substitute for current tax laws, official notices or advice from the Service.

Legal and Institutional Basis

The Nigeria Revenue Service (Establishment) Act, 2025 established the NRS and repealed the Federal Inland Revenue Service (Establishment) Act, 2007. The Act gives the Service responsibilities relating to the assessment, collection and accounting of revenue accruable to the Government of the Federation.

Important name change: many older documents, websites and search results still use “FIRS”. When researching current services, look for the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) and verify information against nrs.gov.ng.

What the Service Does

Revenue administration involves identifying taxable persons and transactions, administering tax laws, collecting revenue, supporting compliance and providing services through which taxpayers can meet their obligations. The NRS performs these functions within the federal revenue framework established by law.

  • Assessing persons and entities that are chargeable with taxes and other applicable revenue.
  • Collecting and accounting for revenue that accrues to the Federation.
  • Administering relevant tax laws and supporting taxpayer compliance.
  • Providing taxpayer registration, filing, payment and verification services.
  • Using digital systems to improve revenue administration and taxpayer interaction.
  • Supporting enforcement, compliance monitoring and revenue assurance within its legal mandate.

Who Uses NRS Services?

Individuals

Individuals may use NRS services to manage tax identification, filing, payments, tax records and related obligations.

Companies

Companies and other organisations interact with the Service for corporate tax administration, filings, payments, compliance and related requirements.

Tax Professionals

Accountants, tax advisers and other authorised representatives may use NRS systems and published guidance when assisting taxpayers.

Public Institutions

Government institutions may interact with the Service on revenue, compliance, reporting and intergovernmental matters provided for by law.

Relationship with Other Government Institutions

The NRS is part of a wider tax and revenue system. Federal, state and other public institutions have different statutory responsibilities. State Internal Revenue Services, for example, administer taxes and levies that fall within state-level jurisdiction.

This distinction matters because not every tax or government charge is administered by the NRS. When a taxpayer receives a demand, notice or payment request, the responsible authority should be identified before payment or submission of personal information.

Former FIRS Name and Current NRS Name

The name “Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS)” remains common in older tax documents, archived web pages, business records and search results. The current statutory institution is the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS).

For historical research, this page retains “FIRS” in its title and explanations so readers can connect older information with the current institution. For current compliance questions, use NRS publications and current tax laws.

How to Find the Correct Tax Information

  1. Identify whether your question concerns federal, state or another tax authority.
  2. Check the current NRS website and relevant tax-law pages.
  3. Use the official taxpayer portal for online services rather than an unofficial intermediary.
  4. Check the date of any circular, notice or guidance because tax rules and procedures can change.
  5. For complex or case-specific matters, obtain professional advice or clarification from the appropriate official channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FIRS still the current name?

No. The current statutory institution is the Nigeria Revenue Service. FIRS is the former name and remains relevant when searching older documents and records.

Where should I access current taxpayer services?

Start from the official NRS website and use the taxpayer-service links provided there. The official Self-Service Portal is available at selfservice.nrs.gov.ng.

Can I rely on an old FIRS webpage for current tax requirements?

Not without checking its date and current status. Tax laws, forms, deadlines and procedures can change, so current NRS publications and applicable legislation should take priority.

Does NRS administer every tax in Nigeria?

No. Different levels and institutions of government have different statutory responsibilities. Confirm which authority has jurisdiction over the particular tax or levy involved.

Is Nigerian Info an official NRS website?

No. Nigerian Info is an independent educational website. Official information should be verified at nrs.gov.ng.

Overview

The Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS) is the federal immigration institution responsible for controlling the movement of persons into and out of Nigeria, issuing travel documents to eligible Nigerians, issuing residence permits to foreigners, conducting border surveillance and patrol, and enforcing immigration laws and regulations within its statutory responsibility.

NIS traces its institutional history to the Immigration Department established in 1963. Its current legal framework was substantially updated by the Immigration Act 2015, which repealed the earlier 1963 legislation and strengthened the Service's structure and responsibilities.

Important: Nigerian Info is an independent educational website. It is not NIS and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Nigeria Immigration Service. Passport, visa, residence, citizenship and border requirements can change; always verify current requirements through the official NIS website before applying or travelling.

What the Nigeria Immigration Service Does

NIS's published core mandate includes:

  • Controlling persons entering or leaving Nigeria.
  • Issuing Nigerian travel documents to eligible Nigerians in Nigeria and abroad.
  • Issuing residence permits to foreign nationals in Nigeria.
  • Conducting border surveillance and patrol.
  • Enforcing immigration laws and regulations for which the Service is responsible.
  • Managing migration-related processes and supporting lawful movement across Nigeria's borders.

Its current organisational structure includes directorates covering passports and travel documents, investigation and compliance, border management, migration, visas and residency, ICT and cyber security, and other administrative functions.

Legal and Institutional Basis

The modern NIS framework is based principally on the Immigration Act 2015. The Act replaced the 1963 legislation and provides the statutory foundation for the Service's immigration-control functions.

NIS also operates through regulations, administrative procedures and service standards. Because immigration requirements can depend on nationality, purpose of travel, document type and individual circumstances, users should consult the current official instructions rather than relying on an old checklist.

Core Functions and Areas of Work

Passport and travel documents

NIS processes Nigerian passports and other travel documents. Its current online system provides application and renewal pathways, with applicants required to complete the relevant process and attend biometric enrolment where instructed.

Visa and entry services

NIS provides visa-related services for foreign nationals travelling to Nigeria, including online application channels for applicable visa categories. Requirements vary by visa type and applicant circumstances.

Border management

The Service is responsible for immigration control at Nigeria's borders, including land, airport and seaport control and border patrol functions. This role concerns lawful entry and exit, immigration compliance and border-management responsibilities.

Migration and residency

NIS manages immigration processes relating to foreign nationals, including residence and migration services. Its official service catalogue includes CERPAC and e-CERPAC services for eligible expatriates.

Investigation and compliance

The Service has investigation, intelligence/surveillance and compliance functions within its organisational structure for enforcing immigration requirements.

Services

NIS currently publishes several service areas through its official website, including:

  • Passports — applications and related passport services.
  • Nigeria visas — visa information and online application services for applicable categories.
  • Convention Travel Document.
  • ECOWAS National Biometric Identity Card services.
  • CERPAC and e-CERPAC services for eligible foreign nationals.
  • Landing and Exit Card services and information.
  • Citizenship and Business services.
  • Temporary Work Permit (TWP) services.
  • Emergency Travel Certificate information for eligible travellers.

Service availability, fees, processing arrangements and eligibility can change. Use the relevant NIS official service page before making payment or submitting personal information.

Who Uses NIS Services?

  • Nigerian citizens applying for or renewing passports and other travel documents.
  • Nigerians abroad who need travel-document or consular-related immigration services.
  • Foreign nationals applying for Nigerian visas or immigration permissions.
  • Foreign nationals requiring residence documentation such as CERPAC, where applicable.
  • Employers and organisations handling immigration requirements for eligible expatriate personnel.
  • Travellers who need current information about entry and exit requirements.

Requirements and Important Information

Immigration requirements are service-specific. A passport application, Nigerian visa application, CERPAC application and temporary work permit do not have the same requirements.

Before applying, check the exact official NIS service page for the document requirements, application steps, payment instructions, appointment or biometric requirements and any country- or category-specific conditions.

Do not pay an intermediary simply because a third party claims to be able to obtain a passport, visa or permit faster. Use official NIS channels and verify payment instructions before providing sensitive personal information.

Relationship to the Federal Government

NIS is a federal government service within Nigeria's internal-affairs and national-security administrative structure. It has a specialised immigration mandate and should not be confused with other agencies that work on border security, customs, policing, citizenship or foreign affairs.

For example, immigration control, customs administration and foreign diplomatic/consular functions are different institutional responsibilities. NIS is specifically concerned with immigration control, travel documents, migration and related border-management functions within its legal mandate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I apply for a Nigerian passport?

Use the passport service available through the official Nigeria Immigration Service website. The current NIS site provides an online application pathway and explains the biometric-enrolment process.

Can I apply for a Nigerian visa online?

For applicable visa categories, NIS provides online visa application services. The correct application route depends on the visa category and applicant's circumstances.

What is CERPAC?

CERPAC is the Comprehensive Expatriate Residence Permit and Aliens Card. NIS provides CERPAC and e-CERPAC services for eligible foreign nationals under the applicable immigration framework.

What does NIS do at Nigeria's borders?

NIS controls persons entering and leaving Nigeria and carries out border surveillance, patrol and immigration-control functions within its statutory mandate.

Where can I check current immigration requirements?

The safest source for current NIS requirements is the official NIS website and the specific service page for the application you intend to make.

Does this page replace official immigration advice?

No. This page is an educational summary. It does not replace NIS instructions, applicable law, official application guidance or professional legal advice where such advice is required.

About the Nigeria Immigration Service

The Nigeria Immigration Service (NIS) is the federal agency responsible for border management, migration control, and the issuance of travel documents in Nigeria. It operates under the Federal Ministry of Interior.

The NIS is tasked with facilitating legitimate travel and trade while preventing illegal immigration, human trafficking, and other cross-border crimes. It manages Nigeria's passport issuance system, visa services, and border surveillance operations.

Core Functions

  • Manage and control Nigeria's international borders.
  • Issue Nigerian passports and travel documents.
  • Process visa applications for foreign nationals.
  • Prevent illegal immigration and human trafficking.
  • Conduct immigration inspections at ports of entry.
  • Enforce immigration laws and regulations.
  • Register and monitor foreign nationals in Nigeria.
  • Facilitate legitimate travel and tourism.
  • Collaborate with international immigration authorities.

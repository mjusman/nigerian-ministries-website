About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Women Affairs is the government ministry responsible for formulating and implementing policies that promote the welfare, rights, and empowerment of women, children, and vulnerable groups in Nigeria.

The Ministry works to eliminate gender-based discrimination, reduce poverty among women, promote access to education and healthcare, and ensure that women fully participate in the social, economic, and political development of the nation.

Core Responsibilities

  • Formulate policies for women's empowerment and gender equality.
  • Promote access to education and skills development for women.
  • Support women's economic empowerment and entrepreneurship.
  • Combat gender-based violence and harmful traditional practices.
  • Promote women's political participation and leadership.
  • Protect the rights of the girl child.
  • Coordinate social welfare programmes for vulnerable women.
  • Collaborate with international organizations on gender issues.
  • Monitor and report on the implementation of gender policies.

Importance to Nigeria

Women constitute about half of Nigeria's population, yet they face significant challenges in accessing education, healthcare, economic opportunities, and political representation. The Ministry of Women Affairs works to address these disparities, promote gender equality, and ensure that women can contribute fully to national development.

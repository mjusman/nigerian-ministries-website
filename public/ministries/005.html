Building Nigeria's Road and Public Infrastructure Network

About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Works is a key ministry of the Federal Government of Nigeria responsible for the planning, construction, rehabilitation, and maintenance of federal roads, bridges, and other public infrastructure.

The ministry works to provide safe, efficient, and sustainable transportation infrastructure that supports economic growth, improves connectivity among states, enhances national development, and promotes public safety.

Vision

To develop and maintain world-class road and public infrastructure that promotes national integration, economic prosperity, and sustainable development across Nigeria.

Mission

To formulate policies, supervise infrastructure projects, ensure quality standards, and provide an efficient road network that meets the needs of citizens and businesses.

Major Responsibilities

  • Construction of federal highways and bridges.
  • Maintenance and rehabilitation of federal roads.
  • Development of national road infrastructure policies.
  • Supervision of public infrastructure projects.
  • Quality assurance and engineering standards.
  • Monitoring road safety infrastructure.
  • Collaboration with state governments on infrastructure development.
  • Promotion of sustainable construction practices.
  • Management of federal public buildings assigned to the ministry.

Key Departments

Department Primary Responsibility
Highways Planning & Development Planning and designing federal road projects.
Highways Construction & Rehabilitation Execution of road and bridge construction projects.
Maintenance Maintenance and repair of federal roads and infrastructure.

Finance & Accounts Budget preparation and financial management.
Human Resources Personnel administration and staff development.
Procurement Transparent procurement of goods, works, and services.

Objectives

  • Improve Nigeria's transportation infrastructure.
  • Reduce travel time across the country.
  • Support trade and economic development.
  • Increase road safety through quality infrastructure.
  • Promote durable and sustainable engineering practices.
  • Strengthen infrastructure management and maintenance.

Importance to National Development

A reliable road network is essential for commerce, agriculture, healthcare, education, security, and tourism. The Federal Ministry of Works plays a strategic role in improving connectivity between cities, facilitating the movement of goods and people, and supporting Nigeria's long-term economic growth.

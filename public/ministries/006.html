About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Transportation is a ministry of the Government of Nigeria responsible for developing, regulating, and coordinating transportation policies across the country. The ministry works to provide safe, efficient, reliable, and sustainable transportation systems that support economic growth, trade, tourism, and national integration.

It oversees transportation by rail, maritime, inland waterways, and related infrastructure while collaborating with other ministries and state governments to improve connectivity throughout Nigeria.

Major Responsibilities

  • Develop national transportation policies and strategies.
  • Coordinate railway development and modernization.
  • Promote maritime safety and shipping activities.
  • Develop inland waterways transportation.
  • Improve transport infrastructure across the country.
  • Enhance cargo movement and logistics.
  • Encourage private sector participation in transportation projects.
  • Support regional and international transport cooperation.

Objectives

  • Provide safe and efficient transport systems.
  • Reduce transportation costs.
  • Improve national connectivity.
  • Support economic diversification.
  • Enhance trade and commerce.
  • Create employment opportunities in the transport sector.
  • Promote environmentally sustainable transportation.

Key Agencies Under the Ministry

Agency Primary Responsibility
Nigerian Railway Corporation (NRC) Railway transportation and infrastructure.
Nigerian Ports Authority (NPA) Management of Nigeria's seaports.
Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency (NIMASA) Maritime safety, security, and shipping regulation.
National Inland Waterways Authority (NIWA) Development and regulation of inland waterways.
Council for the Regulation of Freight Forwarding in Nigeria (CRFFN) Regulation of freight forwarding practice.

Key Areas of Focus

  • Expansion of modern railway networks.
  • Modernization of seaport infrastructure.
  • Development of inland waterways.
  • Improved cargo handling and logistics.
  • Transport safety and security.
  • Public-private partnerships in transport infrastructure.
  • Digital transformation of transport services.

Importance to Nigeria

Transportation is a critical driver of Nigeria's economic development. By improving rail systems, ports, waterways, and logistics services, the Federal Ministry of Transportation helps facilitate trade, reduce travel time, support industrialization, create jobs, and strengthen regional integration within West Africa.

What Is the Federal Ministry of Water Resources & Sanitation?

The Federal Ministry of Water Resources & Sanitation is the federal institution responsible for developing and implementing policies, projects and programmes intended to support sustainable access to safe and sufficient water and related sanitation needs in Nigeria.

The Ministry's official mandate connects water resources management with social, cultural, environmental and economic development. Its work also includes areas such as water supply, dams and reservoirs, irrigation, sanitation and hygiene, hydrological information and cooperation on transboundary water resources. citeturn0search2turn0search0

What the Ministry Does

The Ministry's published mandate and current ministerial information show a broad water-sector role rather than responsibility for every water service in Nigeria. States, local governments, utilities and other institutions also have roles in water and sanitation service delivery.

Water resources planning

Develops policies and strategic plans for integrated and sustainable water resources management.

Dams and reservoirs

Oversees federal responsibilities involving dams, barrages, reservoirs and related hydraulic infrastructure.

Irrigation

Supports irrigation infrastructure and water-resource projects that contribute to agricultural and food-security objectives.

Sanitation and hygiene

Coordinates federal sanitation and hygiene initiatives, including the Clean Nigeria campaign and related programmes.

Water quality and data

Works on water-quality control, hydrological information, research and technical support for water management.

Flood and climate resilience

Supports planning and infrastructure approaches related to flooding, climate resilience and sustainable water management.

Water Supply, Sanitation and Public Health

Water and sanitation are connected to public health, food production, education, environmental protection and economic activity. The Ministry's stated purpose is to support access to safe and sufficient water while maintaining the integrity of freshwater ecosystems. citeturn0search2

Current Ministry initiatives also include sanitation and hygiene, water-supply infrastructure, climate resilience, hydrological data and projects involving dams and irrigation. citeturn0search0turn0search7

For an individual household or community, however, the Ministry may not be the organisation that directly supplies water. The responsible service provider or state/local institution should be identified for a specific local service problem.

Institutions Connected to the Water Sector

The Ministry works within a wider network of federal water institutions. Its official public directory lists multiple River Basin Development Authorities, while other specialised institutions support hydrological information, research and technical functions. citeturn0search4

Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency (NIHSA)

Provides hydrological information and related water-resources data used for planning and assessment.

National Water Resources Institute (NWRI)

Supports training, research and professional development in water resources management.

River Basin Development Authorities

Federal basin institutions involved in water-resource development and related projects across designated river basins.

Institutional mandates and supervisory arrangements can change. Check the Ministry's current official directory when identifying the organisation responsible for a particular project or service.

Why the Ministry Matters

Water-resource decisions affect drinking water, agriculture, sanitation, flood management, ecosystems and economic activity. The Ministry therefore has a role that extends from long-term resource planning to major federal infrastructure and national water-sector coordination.

Recent official publications illustrate this breadth, including the Nigeria National Water Compact 2026–2030, flood-resilience cooperation with Lagos State, sanitation-sector work and groundwater data initiatives. citeturn0search7

Information for Citizens

  • For federal water-resource policies, major projects and Ministry programmes, use the Ministry's official website.
  • For a local water-supply problem, identify the relevant state water authority, local service provider or other responsible institution.
  • For sanitation and hygiene programmes, check current federal and state programme information rather than relying on outdated contact details.
  • For flood or water-related public-safety information, consult the appropriate official emergency, hydrological or state authority for current conditions and instructions.
  • Before making payments or submitting personal information online, verify that the website and contact details belong to the responsible official institution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Ministry directly supply water to every Nigerian household?

No. The federal Ministry has national policy, infrastructure and coordination responsibilities, while water service delivery can involve state and local institutions and utilities.

What areas of water management does the Ministry cover?

Its work includes integrated water resources management, dams and reservoirs, irrigation, water supply, sanitation and hygiene, water quality, hydrological information and related planning.

What are River Basin Development Authorities?

They are federal basin institutions involved in water-resource development and related projects within designated river basins.

Is Nigerian Info an official government website?

No. Nigerian Info is an independent educational resource that explains public institutions and directs readers to official sources.

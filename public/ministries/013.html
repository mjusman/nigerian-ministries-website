About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Water Resources and Sanitation is the government ministry responsible for developing and implementing policies that ensure sustainable access to safe water, adequate sanitation, and efficient water resources management for all Nigerians.

The Ministry oversees water supply projects, dam construction, irrigation schemes, and sanitation programmes across the country. It works to achieve universal access to clean water and improve hygiene practices to enhance public health and productivity.

Core Responsibilities

  • Develop national water resources policies and strategies.
  • Provide urban and rural water supply infrastructure.
  • Promote sanitation and hygiene programmes.
  • Manage dams, reservoirs, and irrigation systems.
  • Protect water catchment areas and watersheds.
  • Coordinate transboundary water resources management.
  • Support climate-resilient water management.
  • Promote integrated water resources management.
  • Monitor water quality and standards.

Importance to Nigeria

Access to clean water and sanitation is fundamental to public health, education, and economic productivity. The Ministry of Water Resources and Sanitation works to provide these essential services, promote sustainable water management, and build resilience against droughts and floods.

What is the Federal Ministry of Finance?

The Federal Ministry of Finance is the federal institution responsible for managing the finances of the Federal Government of Nigeria. Its work includes federal revenue and expenditure management, fiscal policy support, public financial management, resource mobilisation and related aspects of intergovernmental finance.

This page explains the Ministry in plain language and helps readers find the relevant official source. It is an independent educational resource and is not the official website of the Federal Ministry of Finance.

What the Ministry Does

Public finance involves how government raises, manages and spends public resources. The Ministry of Finance provides an important part of the federal framework for this work. According to the Ministry's current official description, its mandate includes managing, controlling and monitoring Federal Government revenues and expenditures while supporting fiscal policy, resource mobilisation, intergovernmental finance and Nigeria's economic relationships.

In practical terms: the Ministry is concerned with the financial systems and policies that help the Federal Government plan revenue, manage expenditure, maintain fiscal discipline and support national development.

Core Responsibilities

  • Managing, controlling and monitoring Federal Government revenue and expenditure.
  • Supporting fiscal policy and public financial management.
  • Supporting resource mobilisation and intergovernmental finance.
  • Contributing to fiscal coordination and government financial planning.
  • Supporting transparency, accountability and efficient management of public resources.
  • Providing financial and economic information through official reports, publications and public resources.

These responsibilities should be understood alongside the roles of other public institutions involved in budgeting, revenue administration, treasury operations, debt management, monetary policy and economic regulation.

Mission and Vision

The Ministry describes its mission around open, transparent, accountable and efficient management of national finances in support of Nigeria's development priorities. Its stated vision links sound public finance with economic stability and improved living standards.

The wording and priorities of government institutions can change over time. Readers should use the Ministry's official website for the current version of its mandate, mission, vision and policy priorities.

Departments and Areas of Work

The Ministry operates through departments covering different parts of its administrative and public-finance responsibilities. Its official website currently lists areas including:

Cash Management

Work relating to government cash management and financial coordination.

Home Finance

Domestic public-finance matters and related financial coordination.

Economic Research & Policy Management

Economic research and policy-management functions.

Finance & Accounts

Internal financial and accounting responsibilities.

International Economic Relations

Economic relationships and international financial engagement.

Legal Services

Legal support for the Ministry's work and institutional responsibilities.

Procurement

Procurement-related administration within the Ministry.

Reform Coordination & Service Improvement

Coordination of reforms and improvements in service delivery.

The official Ministry directory should be consulted for the complete and current list of departments and their published functions.

Institutions Connected to Public Finance

Public finance is not handled by one institution alone. The Federal Ministry of Finance's official directory identifies a range of connected institutions, including the Office of the Accountant-General of the Federation, Debt Management Office, Ministry of Finance Incorporated, Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation, Securities and Exchange Commission and other institutions with specialised mandates.

Their responsibilities are distinct, so a person looking for a service or document should confirm which institution has the relevant mandate rather than assuming that every financial matter is handled directly by the Ministry.

Information and Services for Citizens

The Ministry's official website provides access to public-finance resources, including budget and expenditure information, Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) and federation-revenue information, fiscal-policy material, reports and publications, and information about programmes and initiatives.

If you are trying to find a government financial document, begin by identifying the subject—such as budget information, revenue, expenditure, debt or fiscal policy—and then use the Ministry's official resources to find the responsible office or institution.

  • Researchers and students studying public finance or fiscal policy.

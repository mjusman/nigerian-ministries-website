About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Petroleum Resources is the government ministry responsible for formulating and implementing policies that guide the exploration, production, refining, marketing, and distribution of petroleum resources in Nigeria. It oversees the nation's oil and gas sector, which is the backbone of the Nigerian economy.

The Ministry works to ensure the sustainable development of Nigeria's hydrocarbon resources, attract investment in the energy sector, promote local content, and maximize revenue generation for national development.

Vision

To be a world-class petroleum administration that ensures the sustainable development of Nigeria's oil and gas resources for the benefit of all Nigerians.

Mission

To formulate and implement policies that promote efficient exploration, production, refining, and distribution of petroleum resources while ensuring environmental sustainability, transparency, and value maximization.

Core Responsibilities

  • Formulate national petroleum policies and strategies.
  • Regulate and supervise the oil and gas industry.
  • Promote local content development in the petroleum sector.
  • Oversee the implementation of the Petroleum Industry Act (PIA).
  • Negotiate and manage international oil agreements.
  • Ensure environmental protection in petroleum operations.
  • Promote gas development and commercialization.
  • Monitor petroleum product supply and distribution.

Importance to Nigeria

The petroleum sector contributes significantly to Nigeria's GDP, government revenue, and foreign exchange earnings. The Ministry of Petroleum Resources plays a critical role in ensuring the efficient management of these resources, attracting investment, promoting transparency, and driving the transition to cleaner energy.

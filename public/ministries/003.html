About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Health and Social Welfare (FMoHSW) is responsible for developing national health policies, coordinating healthcare services, and improving the health and well-being of Nigerians. The Ministry works with state governments, development partners, healthcare professionals, and other stakeholders to strengthen the country's health system and ensure access to quality healthcare services.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop national health policies and strategic plans.
  • Coordinate public healthcare delivery across Nigeria.
  • Improve maternal, newborn, and child health.
  • Control communicable and non-communicable diseases.
  • Strengthen primary healthcare services.
  • Regulate healthcare standards and medical practice.
  • Promote health education and disease prevention.
  • Coordinate emergency preparedness and response.
  • Support healthcare workforce development.

Major Agencies Under the Ministry

NCDC

The Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention is responsible for disease surveillance, outbreak detection, and emergency response.

NAFDAC

Regulates and controls the manufacture, importation, exportation, distribution, advertisement, and use of food, drugs, cosmetics, medical devices, and chemicals.

NHIA

The National Health Insurance Authority promotes access to affordable healthcare through health insurance schemes.

Primary Healthcare Development Agency

Coordinates the development and strengthening of primary healthcare services throughout Nigeria.

Strategic Goals

  • Achieve Universal Health Coverage (UHC).
  • Reduce maternal and child mortality.
  • Improve access to quality healthcare.
  • Strengthen healthcare infrastructure.
  • Expand immunization coverage.
  • Promote digital health and innovation.
  • Improve disease prevention and health promotion.
  • Ensure affordable medicines and medical supplies.

Vision

To build a healthy Nigeria where every citizen has access to affordable, quality, and equitable healthcare services.

Mission

To formulate and implement policies that strengthen Nigeria's health system, improve healthcare outcomes, promote disease prevention, and enhance the overall well-being of all Nigerians through effective leadership and collaboration.

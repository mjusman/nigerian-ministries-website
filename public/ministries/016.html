About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Labour and Employment is the government ministry responsible for formulating and implementing policies that promote decent work, employment generation, industrial harmony, and the protection of workers' rights in Nigeria.

The Ministry oversees labour administration, employment services, occupational safety and health, and industrial relations. It works to create an enabling environment for job creation, skills development, and fair labour practices.

Core Responsibilities

  • Formulate national labour and employment policies.
  • Promote employment generation and job creation.
  • Protect workers' rights and ensure fair labour practices.
  • Mediate industrial disputes and promote harmony.
  • Enforce occupational safety and health standards.
  • Oversee skills development and vocational training.
  • Manage labour migration and international labour matters.
  • Register and regulate trade unions and employers' associations.
  • Coordinate the National Labour Advisory Council.

Importance to Nigeria

The Ministry of Labour and Employment plays a vital role in promoting decent work, reducing unemployment, and maintaining industrial peace. By protecting workers' rights, promoting skills development, and facilitating job creation, the Ministry contributes to economic growth, social stability, and improved living standards for Nigerian workers.

About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Justice is the government ministry headed by the Attorney General of the Federation (AGF). It serves as the chief law office of the Federal Government of Nigeria, providing legal advice, drafting legislation, prosecuting criminal cases, and representing the government in legal matters.

The Ministry is responsible for upholding the rule of law, protecting the rights of citizens, and ensuring that government actions comply with the Constitution and laws of Nigeria.

Vision

To be a model justice ministry that upholds the rule of law, protects citizens' rights, and promotes justice, fairness, and transparency in the administration of justice in Nigeria.

Mission

To provide efficient legal services to the government, protect the public interest, promote access to justice, and strengthen the legal and judicial system in Nigeria.

Core Responsibilities

  • Provide legal advice to the Federal Government.
  • Draft and review legislation for the National Assembly.
  • Prosecute federal criminal cases.
  • Represent the government in civil litigation.
  • Promote access to justice and legal aid.
  • Protect citizens' fundamental human rights.
  • Coordinate the administration of criminal justice.
  • Manage the federal legal system and law reform.
  • Oversee extradition and mutual legal assistance.

Importance to Nigeria

The Federal Ministry of Justice plays a vital role in ensuring that the Nigerian government operates within the law, that citizens have access to justice, and that the legal system is fair, efficient, and transparent. It is a cornerstone of Nigeria's democracy and governance structure.

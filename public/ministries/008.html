About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Power is the government ministry responsible for developing and implementing policies that ensure the generation, transmission, and distribution of adequate, reliable, and affordable electricity across Nigeria.

The Ministry oversees the power sector, including thermal and hydroelectric power plants, the national grid, renewable energy programmes, and rural electrification projects. It works to improve electricity access, attract private investment, and modernize the nation's power infrastructure.

Vision

To achieve universal access to reliable, affordable, and sustainable electricity for all Nigerians by expanding generation capacity, strengthening transmission infrastructure, and improving distribution efficiency.

Mission

To formulate policies and strategies that accelerate power sector development, promote energy mix diversification, enhance grid stability, and drive electrification of rural and underserved communities.

Core Responsibilities

  • Develop national power sector policies and strategies.
  • Oversee electricity generation, transmission, and distribution.
  • Promote renewable energy and clean power technologies.
  • Coordinate rural electrification programmes.
  • Attract private sector investment in the power sector.
  • Monitor and evaluate power sector performance.
  • Support grid expansion and modernization.
  • Promote energy efficiency and conservation.

Importance to Nigeria

Access to reliable electricity is essential for industrialization, healthcare, education, technology, and overall quality of life. The Federal Ministry of Power is at the forefront of Nigeria's efforts to overcome its power challenges, expand energy access, and create an enabling environment for businesses and households to thrive.

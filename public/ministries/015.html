What Is the Federal Ministry of Youth Development?

The Federal Ministry of Youth Development (FMYD) is the federal ministry responsible for providing a framework for the formulation and implementation of policies, programmes and initiatives that promote and enhance youth development in collaboration with stakeholders.

The ministry is now a distinct youth-development institution. The ministry's official history explains that youth development and sports were previously combined, but the federal government subsequently separated the youth-development function from sports so that youth-specific policy and programmes could receive dedicated attention.

Independent information: Nigerian Info is not the Federal Ministry of Youth Development and is not affiliated with the Nigerian government. Confirm current programmes, application requirements, contacts and deadlines on the ministry's official website before taking action.

Mandate and Core Responsibilities

The ministry states that its mandate includes the formulation, implementation, monitoring and evaluation of policies and programmes on youth development, with emphasis on areas such as wealth creation, youth empowerment, wellbeing, excellence, national unity and sustainable development.

  • Develop and coordinate policies and programmes focused on Nigerian youth.
  • Support youth empowerment, skills development, entrepreneurship and opportunities for productive participation.
  • Monitor and evaluate youth-development programmes and initiatives.
  • Promote collaboration among government institutions, private-sector organisations, civil society and development partners.
  • Support youth participation, leadership, inclusion and engagement in national development.
  • Provide or coordinate information about opportunities, programmes, training and other youth-focused initiatives.
  • Work with relevant institutions on issues affecting youth wellbeing and social and economic development.

The ministry's responsibilities should not be confused with the work of every organisation that offers services to young Nigerians. Many programmes and services involve other federal institutions, state governments, local authorities or partner organisations.

Why the Ministry Matters

Youth policy cuts across education, employment, entrepreneurship, digital skills, civic participation, health, social inclusion and economic development. A dedicated federal ministry provides a central policy and coordination point for these youth-development concerns.

For citizens, this distinction is useful: the ministry may formulate or coordinate policy and programmes, while another government institution may actually administer a particular service, funding mechanism, training programme or statutory scheme.

Major Areas of Work

Skills and employability

The ministry identifies skills development and pathways into employment as important parts of its youth-development agenda, including digital, practical and employability skills.

Entrepreneurship and enterprise development

Youth entrepreneurship and access to business support are among the ministry's current focus areas. Its official initiatives include support involving training, mentorship, enterprise development and access to opportunities.

Youth participation and leadership

The ministry's strategic work includes youth engagement and participation in governance and national development, alongside initiatives intended to create opportunities for young people to contribute to public life.

Wellbeing and social inclusion

The ministry's two-year strategic plan identifies youth wellbeing and social inclusion among its areas of focus, alongside skills, employment and entrepreneurship.

Coordination and partnerships

Youth development requires cooperation across government and with private-sector, civil-society and development organisations. The ministry's mandate explicitly places collaboration with stakeholders at the centre of its work.

Current Youth Initiatives and Programmes

Programme names, eligibility rules and application windows can change. The following are examples of initiatives currently described by the ministry's official website, rather than a permanent list of all government opportunities.

Nigerian Youth Academy (NiYA)

NiYA is a youth-focused programme of the Federal Ministry of Youth Development that brings together learning, employment, entrepreneurship, innovation and community opportunities. The ministry's NiYA page describes a goal of expanding skills and opportunities for young Nigerians and currently lists startup and founder-support activities.

Visit the official NiYA page.

Youth Data Protection Awareness and Training (YDPAT)

YDPAT is an initiative of the Federal Ministry of Youth Development and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission focused on data-protection awareness, digital skills and online safety for young people.

Learn about YDPAT on the official ministry site.

Other current initiatives

The ministry's website also publishes information about youth entrepreneurship, health awareness, funding and skills-related initiatives. Because application conditions and programme status may change, citizens should use the ministry's current news and initiative pages rather than relying on an old opportunity list.

Strategic Plan

The Federal Ministry of Youth Development has published a two-year strategic plan built around Support, Empower and Protect (SEP). The plan addresses youth skills, employment, entrepreneurship, participation, wellbeing and social inclusion and provides a framework for the ministry's programme direction.

Read the official two-year Youth Development Strategic Plan.

Information for Young Nigerians

Who may need the ministry?

Young Nigerians, youth-led organisations, entrepreneurs, researchers, civil-society groups, development partners and public institutions may need the ministry for information about youth policy, empowerment initiatives, skills, entrepreneurship, participation and related programmes.

Before applying for a programme

  • Check that the opportunity appears on an official government website or an official partner channel.
  • Read the current eligibility requirements, application period and supporting-document requirements.
  • Do not pay an individual merely because they claim to be able to secure a place in a government programme.
  • Use the official contact details provided by the ministry when you need clarification.
  • Keep copies of applications, reference numbers and official correspondence.

Finding the right institution

Not every youth-related issue belongs to the Ministry of Youth Development. Education, employment regulation, health, taxation, national service, student finance and other matters may involve different ministries or agencies. Use our government structure guide and the relevant institution pages to identify the appropriate organisation.

Relationship with Sports and the NYSC

The current Federal Ministry of Youth Development is separate from the federal sports administration. The ministry's official history explains the earlier merger of youth and sports responsibilities and the subsequent restructuring that established a distinct youth-development ministry.

The ministry also works on matters connected to the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC). In 2026, the ministry reported federal-level work relating to proposed NYSC reforms. For current NYSC services and application procedures, citizens should also consult the NYSC's own official channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Ministry of Youth Development still combined with sports?

No. The ministry's current official website presents the Federal Ministry of Youth Development as a separate institution focused on youth development.

Does the ministry give money to every young entrepreneur who applies?

No general entitlement should be assumed. Individual initiatives have their own eligibility, selection and funding rules. Check the official programme page for the current conditions.

What is NiYA?

NiYA, the Nigerian Youth Academy, is a Federal Ministry of Youth Development programme focused on learning, employment, entrepreneurship, innovation and related opportunities for young Nigerians.

Where can I find current youth opportunities?

The ministry's official website, initiative pages and news section are the appropriate places to check for current announcements, application windows and programme updates.

Can the ministry handle every youth-related complaint?

No. The correct institution depends on the issue. Some matters fall under other federal agencies, state governments, local authorities or specialised public institutions.

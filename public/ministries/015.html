About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Youth and Sports Development is the government ministry responsible for formulating and implementing policies that promote youth development, empowerment, and sports excellence in Nigeria.

The Ministry works to harness the potential of Nigeria's youth population through skills development, entrepreneurship programmes, and civic engagement. It also oversees the development of sports at all levels, from grassroots to professional, and manages national sports federations and athletes.

Core Responsibilities

  • Develop national youth and sports policies.
  • Promote youth empowerment and entrepreneurship.
  • Coordinate youth skills development programmes.
  • Oversee national sports federations and associations.
  • Develop sports infrastructure across Nigeria.
  • Support Nigerian athletes and sports professionals.
  • Promote grassroots sports development.
  • Coordinate national youth service programmes.
  • Foster international sports cooperation.

Importance to Nigeria

Nigeria has one of the largest youth populations in the world. The Ministry of Youth and Sports Development plays a critical role in empowering young people with skills, opportunities, and platforms to succeed. Through sports, the Ministry promotes national unity, healthy living, and international recognition for Nigeria.

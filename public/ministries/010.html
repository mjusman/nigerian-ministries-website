About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Defence is the government ministry responsible for formulating and implementing defence policies to safeguard Nigeria's sovereignty, territorial integrity, and national interests. It oversees the Nigerian Armed Forces and coordinates defence and security operations.

The Ministry ensures that the military is adequately equipped, trained, and prepared to respond to internal and external threats, including insurgency, terrorism, banditry, and other security challenges.

Core Responsibilities

  • Formulate national defence and security policies.
  • Oversee the Nigerian Armed Forces (Army, Navy, Air Force).
  • Coordinate military operations and national security.
  • Manage defence budget and procurement.
  • Ensure military readiness and capacity building.
  • Promote defence research and technology.
  • Coordinate peacekeeping operations.
  • Collaborate with international defence partners.
  • Provide welfare and support for military personnel.

Importance to Nigeria

The Ministry of Defence is critical to Nigeria's stability and security. It protects the nation from external aggression, supports internal security operations, contributes to international peacekeeping, and ensures that the Armed Forces remain professional, capable, and responsive to emerging threats.

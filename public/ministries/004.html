About the Ministry

The Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security (FMAFS) is the government institution responsible for developing and implementing policies that promote agriculture, food production, and national food security in Nigeria. The ministry works to improve agricultural productivity, increase farmers' incomes, and ensure sustainable management of natural resources.

Vision: To achieve sustainable agricultural growth, food security, wealth creation, and economic development for Nigeria.

Key Functions

  • Develop agricultural policies and strategies.
  • Promote food security and nutrition.
  • Support crop, livestock, and fisheries development.
  • Provide agricultural extension services.
  • Facilitate agricultural research and innovation.
  • Promote mechanized and climate-smart agriculture.
  • Encourage private sector investment in agriculture.
  • Coordinate agricultural development programmes across Nigeria.

Objectives

  • Increase food production nationwide.
  • Create employment opportunities through agriculture.
  • Reduce poverty among rural communities.
  • Enhance agricultural exports.
  • Improve access to modern farming technologies.
  • Strengthen food supply chains and storage systems.

Major Areas of Focus

  • Crop Production
  • Livestock Development
  • Fisheries and Aquaculture
  • Agricultural Extension Services
  • Food Security Initiatives
  • Rural Development
  • Irrigation and Water Management
  • Climate-Smart Agriculture
